1. Product overview & users

FixoraX is a local-services marketplace for India. One app, two sides:

  • Customer — “I need help”: posts a task (category, address, details, budget in ₹), gets offers from nearby helpers, chooses one, chats, marks it done.
  • Helper — “I want to help”: sees open jobs, sends an offer (own price) or accepts at the customer’s price, chats once chosen.

Login is by mobile number + OTP or Google. Guests may browse; posting or offering asks them to log in. Tasks, offers and chats are shared between phones through Firebase (Cloud Firestore) once the project is set up.

Design target

Bharat-first: Tier-2/3 towns, budget Android phones (3–4 GB RAM), weak networks, users who prefer Hindi or Hinglish, and older users with large font sizes.

2. Design principles

1 · One clear action per screen

Each screen has one blue primary button (56 dp tall). Secondary actions are outlined or text buttons.

2 · Plain, honest words

“Enter OTP”, “Send offer”, “Wrong OTP… 2 tries left”. No jargon such as “Authenticate”. No fake numbers, ratings or badges.

3 · No passwords

Mobile number + OTP or Google. New and returning users use the same screens; the account is created automatically.

4 · Readable for everyone

Navy text on white (16:1). Minimum text 12 sp. Layouts grow with the phone’s font size instead of clipping.

5 · Works on weak networks

Changes are saved on the phone at once and sent when the network returns; lists show the last known data; buttons never spin forever.

6 · Indian formats

+91 always visible, number cleaned automatically, ₹ with Indian grouping (₹1,25,000), dates DD/MM/YYYY in IST.

7 · Native Android feel

Material 3 components, edge-to-edge with safe areas, system Back closes sheets/chats first, “Press back again to exit” on the first tab.

8 · Hindi-ready

All texts live in one file (strings.xml). A Hindi version is drafted for review; layouts were checked with it.

8. Screens: login

Welcome → Mobile number → OTP → Name → Choose side

8.1 Welcome

First screen for new or logged-out users. Two equal ways to continue, plus browsing without login.

What is on the screen

  • English / हिंदी choice at the top (Hindi in preparation)
  • Illustration that shrinks on small phones
  • “Welcome to FixoraX” and one-line promise
  • Continue with mobile number (primary) and Continue with Google (official G logo)
  • Explore without login
  • Terms and Privacy line (links appear once the pages exist)

States

  • Google busy: spinner in the Google button
  • Google problem: plain message under the buttons (e.g. no Google account on the phone)

Design notes

  • Replaces the old Sign In / Create Account / password screens
  • Whole screen scrolls at large font sizes

8.2 Mobile number

Enter or pick the mobile number to receive an OTP. Same screen for new and returning users.

What is on the screen

  • +91 always visible
  • Number pad keyboard; spaces, dashes, leading 0 and +91 removed automatically
  • Pick number from my SIM — Google’s phone-number hint, no permission needed, shows both SIMs
  • Send OTP with spinner while sending
  • “No password needed.”

States

  • Error under the field: “Enter your 10-digit mobile number” / “should start with 6, 7, 8 or 9”
  • Too many tries, no internet: plain messages
  • Test mode: yellow note “Use OTP 123456” (only until Firebase is set up)

8.3 Enter OTP

Type, paste or auto-read the 6-digit code.

What is on the screen

  • “Sent by SMS to +91 98765 43210” and Change number
  • Six OTP boxes (one real input)
  • Resend OTP in 30 s, then Resend OTP
  • Verify — checks automatically when 6 digits are entered

States

  • Auto-filled from SMS: green tick “OTP filled automatically from your SMS”
  • Wrong OTP: red boxes and “2 tries left”; after 3 → resend required
  • Expired code: “This OTP has expired. Tap Resend OTP”

Design notes

  • Survives the app being closed while the user reads the SMS (number and code request are saved)

8.4 Name

Asked only once, after the first login. The only profile question.

What is on the screen

  • “What should we call you?”
  • One field — a single name is fine (no surname required)
  • Continue

States

  • Less than 2 letters: “Please enter your name (at least 2 letters)”

Design notes

  • Replaces the old 7-field profile (gender, date of birth, state, PIN…)

8.5 Choose your side

Shown once; the choice is remembered. Can be changed later from Account.

What is on the screen

  • FixoraX logo
  • Two large cards: I need help (blue) and I want to help (orange) with illustrations
  • English / हिंदी pills (this screen already has its own Hindi)

Design notes

  • Cards grow with large text instead of cutting it off

11. Screens: chat & account

11.1 Chats

One conversation per task, between the customer and the chosen helper only.

What is on the screen

  • List: avatar, other person’s name, task title
  • Tap to open

States

  • Empty (customer): “Chats appear here after you choose a helper.”
  • Empty (helper): “Chats appear here after a customer chooses you.”

11.2 Conversation

Simple messaging; works on weak networks (messages are sent when the network returns).

What is on the screen

  • Header: name, task and agreed price
  • My messages blue on the right, theirs white on the left, with IST time (e.g. 7:59 am)
  • Message box with Send
  • Helpers get quick replies (“I can do this today.”, “I’ll arrive in 20 minutes.”)

Design notes

  • System Back closes the conversation first

11.3 Account

Profile and everything about the account in one place.

What is on the screen

  • Profile card: initial avatar, name, number, Edit
  • Language / भाषा
  • Switch to “I want to help” / “I need help”
  • Help: WhatsApp, Call support, Common questions (appear once the numbers/links are set)
  • Legal: Privacy Policy, Terms (appear once the pages exist)
  • Your account: Log out · Delete my account (red, asks to confirm)
  • App version

States

  • Guest: “You are exploring without login” + Log in
  • Delete needs a recent login: “please log out, log in again, then delete”

Design notes

  • Log out and delete both remove this phone’s tasks, offers, chats and photos (shared family phones)

12. States: empty, error, offline, guest

StateRule
LoadingGrey skeleton cards that look like the content (no full-screen spinners); a spinner only inside the button being pressed.
EmptyIcon, one plain sentence, and one action (e.g. Post a task / See jobs near you).
ErrorSays what happened and what to do next, next to the place it happened. Input is kept.
Offline / weak networkLast loaded data stays visible; new posts, offers and messages are saved on the phone and sent later.
GuestCan browse; posting a task or sending an offer opens “Log in to continue” (mobile number or Google, “Not now”).
Test modeBefore Firebase is set up: a yellow note on login screens and in Account. Never shown once real login is on.

13. Tablets & foldables

On tablets, unfolded foldables and landscape, content keeps a readable maximum width of 640 dp and is centred instead of stretching buttons across the screen. Android 16 ignores portrait locks on large screens, so the app does not rely on one.

14. Hindi (draft, for review)

All 247 texts have a simple-Hindi draft (docs/i18n/HINDI_REVIEW.md). Everyday words stay in English (OTP, SMS, Google, Online, Chat, WhatsApp). The draft is not yet in the app — it goes in after the owner or a native speaker approves it. These screenshots confirm the layouts fit Hindi text.
